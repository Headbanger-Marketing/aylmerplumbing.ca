FAQs

Questions Aylmer Homeowners Ask

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Are you licensed for plumbing work around Aylmer?

Yes. Aylmer Plumbing holds a plumbing licence, and jobs requiring permits are filed with the proper Elgin authority so your work passes inspection and stays insurable.

Do you take emergency calls in the Aylmer area?

We do. Frozen lines, bursts, and backed-up sewers around Aylmer and Malahide get priority dispatch, including evenings through the winter.

How quickly can a failed water heater be replaced?

Faster than most expect. Common tank sizes ride on the truck, so an Aylmer replacement usually wraps in one visit with the old unit hauled away.

Our farmhouse still has galvanized pipe. Should we repipe?

When baths run orange and pressure drops as fixtures open, the galvanized is done. We can repipe the whole Elgin farmhouse or stage it wing by wing to fit the budget.

Do you work on wells and septic systems?

That is everyday work here. Pressure tanks, well switches, yard lines to barns, and drain runs to septic beds around Aylmer are all within scope, and we clean lines with methods that respect the leaching bed.

Is Aylmer water hard enough to need a softener?

Village supply runs hard, and area wells often add iron or sulphur on top of it. A test tells us exactly what your fixtures are fighting, and we size treatment to that result.

What should plumbing work cost?

You will see the number in writing before we start, with the diagnosis behind it explained plainly. Repair-versus-replace options come side by side so you choose with full information.

How far outside Aylmer will you travel?

Across Malahide and the surrounding Elgin concessions, including Springfield, Port Burwell, Vienna, and over toward Tillsonburg and St. Thomas.

Do you serve areas outside of Aylmer, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Aylmer and nearby communities including Springfield, Port Burwell, Vienna, Malahide, and the surrounding area.
